FlipFest

A weekend retreat at Flip Fest Gymnastics Camp in Crossville, TN. Open to all adults 18+ regardless of background or skill level.

What's included

  • Three practice sessions (Friday evening, Saturday morning & afternoon)
  • Cabin lodging + all meals
  • Lake activities, ropes course, campfires, games
  • Coaching from UCG staff and visiting clinicians

FlipStar

UCG's fall training intensive — three days of focused skill work with clinicians, in a smaller-group format than FlipFest.

FAQ: Retreats

What should I pack?

Gym attire for 3–4 practice sessions, cabin essentials (sheets, blanket, toiletries), bathing suit, bug spray, sunscreen, an outfit for the bonfire, and any tape or braces you use.

Can I bring a friend who isn't a UCG member?

You need an active UCG individual membership to attend FlipFest or FlipStar. They can register the same day they sign up for the retreat.

What's the skill range?

FlipFest welcomes everyone from rusty beginners to advanced competitors. FlipStar is built for Level 5+ athletes working on specific skill goals.

Are refunds available?

Retreat fees are refundable up to 30 days before the event minus a $25 processing fee. After that, fees are non-refundable but transferable to another UCG member.
