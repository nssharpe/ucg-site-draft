Host a meet.

Run a sanctioned competition at your club — the playbook, the forms, and how to find judges.

Competition OverviewNationalsMastersSanctioned EventsRetreatsRules & CoPHost a MeetRegistration HelpTraining Resources

Hosting a meet is one of the most rewarding things a UCG club can do. This page walks through the full process — including how to get sanctioned, find judges, and make life easier for visiting teams.

The 5-step playbook

1. Pick a date

Coordinate with your gym schedule. Avoid spring break and major regional championships. Most clubs lock in dates over the summer for the following season.

2. Decide on sanctioning

Sanctioned meets get free UCG awards, access to ScoreFlippers for registration and scoring, discounted hotel bookings for visiting teams, and listing on the official events calendar. In exchange you commit to certified judges, the current rule book, and standard safety practices.

Submit your sanction request at least 8 weeks before competition.

3. Submit the form

Two paths:

  • Sanction Request Form — reviewed and posted within ~2 weeks
  • Non-sanctioned Event Listing — appears on the calendar immediately

4. Secure judges and an athletic trainer

Request judges by October 15 if you're hosting in the spring. UCG maintains a state-by-state judges directory and has a request form to broadcast your need.

5. Promote

Your regional rep can broadcast to nearby clubs. Most clubs also do social media outreach and direct invites to teams they've competed against before.

Tips that pay off

  • Offer multiple levels with separated rotations, preferably in ascending difficulty order.
  • Stagger MAG and WAG so teams can share equipment efficiently.
  • Brief your judges on UCG-specific rules — the modifications from USAG are documented but easy to miss.
  • For UAG events, make athletes responsible for arriving when their events occur — the format moves fast.
