Common situations

I'm an individual — how do I register for Nationals?

Make sure your individual membership is active for the current season. Then log into ScoreFlippers and find "UCG Nationals 2026" in the events list. Select your discipline and level for each event you're competing.

I'm a club admin — how do I register my whole team?

From your club admin dashboard in ScoreFlippers, click "Bulk register athletes." Upload the roster CSV or check boxes for each athlete. You'll be billed once for the whole team.

What's the difference between sanctioned and non-sanctioned events?

Sanctioned events use UCG rules and judges, and scores count toward UCG records. Non-sanctioned events appear on the calendar but use their own rules and structure.

I missed registration — can I sign up late?

For most events, yes — late registration is available for an extra fee until ~2 weeks before competition. Nationals late registration runs Feb 15 – Mar 1 with a $25 surcharge.

How do refunds work?

Refund policy varies by event. Most sanctioned meets allow full refunds up to 14 days before competition. Nationals refunds end Feb 14. Check the event page for the specific policy.

I don't see my club in ScoreFlippers.

Your club's annual membership probably hasn't renewed yet. Email your club admin or have them reach out to info@ucg.org.

Still stuck?

Email info@ucg.org — include your name, club, and event. We typically reply within 24 hours.
