How the season works

Local meets → Regionals → Nationals

The UCG season runs roughly September through April. Most athletes compete at a handful of local meets before regional and national championships.

Sep – Feb

1. Local meets

Clubs host (and travel to) sanctioned dual meets, invitationals, and conference championships throughout the fall and winter.

Season calendar
Feb – Mar

2. Regionals

Eight regional championships across the country — a great mid-season target for newer competitors and teams.

Regional meets
Apr

3. Nationals

Three days of preliminary competition and finals across every discipline. Open to all UCG members — no qualification required.

Nationals hub
May – Aug

4. Off-season

Recovery, training camps, FlipFest, Masters competitions, and rule revisions for the next year.

Retreats

Nationals 2026 — Birmingham, AL

April 8–11, 2026. The largest adult gymnastics championship in the country returns to the South. Three days of preliminary competition, one session of finals, and every discipline UCG offers.
