Every UCG discipline has its own rule book, code of points, and scoring tools. Pick a discipline below for the full rule package, or browse the cross-cutting reference materials.

WAG

Women's Artistic Gymnastics

Vault, bars, beam, floor. Four levels — Xcel Silver, Platinum, Diamond, and Level 9 — plus Open Scoring at Nationals.

Open WAG rules MAG

Men's Artistic Gymnastics

Six events across Developmental, Intermediate, and Advanced — FIG Code of Points with UCG modifications.

Open MAG rules T&T

Trampoline & Tumbling

Individual Trampoline, Double Mini, Power Tumbling, and Synchronized — across New Flyers, Intermediate, and High Flyers.

Open T&T rules UAG

UAG · Decathlon & Omnithon

Mix any WAG and MAG apparatus at any level. Decathlon covers all 10 events; Omnithon is the shorter format.

Open UAG rules
Masters

Masters Division

Modified scoring built for athletes returning to the sport.

Masters Rules (PDF) Masters hub

Format

Athletes self-select skill level. Routines are scored on execution and presentation; difficulty bonuses are available but not required. The bracket is collegial — focus is on showing up, not on the medal.
