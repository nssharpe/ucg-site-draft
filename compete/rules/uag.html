UAG, Decathlon & Omnithon rules.

UCG's signature format — any WAG or MAG apparatus, at any level, for any athlete.

Overview WAG MAG T&T UAG / Decathlon Masters

UAG — Unified Artistic Gymnastics — is UCG's signature discipline. Gymnasts can compete on any apparatus across WAG and MAG events, regardless of gender or sex. Decathlon covers all ten events; Omnithon is the shorter format.

Apparatus

WAG apparatus

  • Vault
  • Uneven Bars
  • Balance Beam
  • Floor Exercise

MAG apparatus

  • Floor Exercise
  • Pommel Horse
  • Rings
  • Vault
  • Parallel Bars
  • High Bar

Key rules

Level flexibility

Gymnasts may combine any level of WAG with any level of MAG — but must stick to a single level within each discipline.

Decathlon scoring

Decathlon score = 5 × WAG All-Around + MAG All-Around

This formula is calibrated to keep WAG-trained and MAG-trained athletes on roughly equal footing across the full ten-event combined score.

Competition format

Preliminaries separate WAG and MAG sessions. Gymnasts are responsible for managing their own schedule across both sessions without delaying competition.

Nationals

  • Single registration fee covers all UAG competition disciplines
  • Awards by participant count per level combination:
    • 6+ athletes — 3 awards
    • 1–5 athletes — 1 award

Gender categories

Participants select Male, Female, or Other at registration. Those selecting Other receive a brief post-registration survey to indicate Men+ or Women+ category preference for awards purposes.
