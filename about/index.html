For the Love of the Sport — since 1989.

UCG is a volunteer-run national community that's been making gymnastics possible for college students and adults for more than three decades. Here's who we are, what we do, and how we got here.

Our Story

A nonprofit built by gymnasts, for gymnasts.

United Club Gymnastics (UCG) — formerly the National Association of Intercollegiate Gymnastics Clubs (NAIGC) — exists for one reason: to make sure the sport doesn't end after high school.

What started in 1989 as a single national championship for college club teams has grown into a year-round national organization. We sanction local and regional meets, write the rules our community competes by, run the largest adult gymnastics championship in the country, fund scholarships, and connect more than 2,600 athletes across 150+ clubs from coast to coast.

"To provide collegiate and adult gymnastics opportunities through competition, community building, and pushing the boundaries of the sport."

What we believe

  • Everyone belongs on the floor. Inclusive participation across skill levels, ages, identities, and disciplines.
  • The athlete experience comes first. Every rule change, sanctioning decision, and event is judged against the experience it creates.
  • Open by default. Transparent governance, published meeting minutes, and elected leadership.
  • Innovation belongs in gymnastics. Our Unified Artistic format — Decathlon and Omnithon — exists nowhere else in the sport.

A short timeline

  • 1989 — First national championships.
  • 2006 — Formally incorporated as a nonprofit.
  • 2008 — Awarded 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status.
  • 2015 — Unified Artistic Gymnastics (UAG) introduced.
  • 2020 — Masters division launched, opening competition to athletes returning to the sport later in life.
  • 2024 — Surpassed 1,800 active members.
  • 2026 — Rebrand from NAIGC to United Club Gymnastics.

For our alumni

Once a UCG athlete, always part of the community. The Alumni Network keeps former competitors connected to the sport — through mentorship opportunities, the Masters division, judging certification, volunteer roles on the Operations Leadership Team, and an open invitation back to Nationals every year.

The rebrand

Why UCG? Three reasons. NAIGC never quite said what we did — we're not only intercollegiate, and many of our members aren't in college at all. United reflects the way our community shows up for each other: across disciplines, schools, ages, and identities. And Club Gymnastics is exactly what we do — gymnastics organized at the club level, by the athletes themselves. The motto stays. For the Love of the Sport.
