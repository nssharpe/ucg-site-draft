What we compete

Five disciplines, one community.

UCG sanctions five competitive disciplines at sanctioned meets and Nationals. Each has its own rule book and difficulty levels — and our signature Unified Artistic format exists nowhere else in the sport.

WAG

Women's Artistic Gymnastics

Vault, uneven bars, balance beam, and floor exercise. Nine difficulty levels (Levels 1–9) accommodate beginners through ex-elites.

  • Largest discipline by membership
  • USAG-derived skill progressions
  • Team and all-around competition formats
WAG rules & CoP
MAG

Men's Artistic Gymnastics

Floor, pommel horse, rings, vault, parallel bars, and high bar. Multiple levels including our innovative Decathlon division.

  • All six men's events
  • Open routine construction at upper levels
  • Modified event options for development
MAG rules & CoP
UAG

Unified Artistic Gymnastics

Our signature format. Decathlon and Omnithon competitions blend WAG and MAG events into a single all-around — open to any athlete regardless of gender.

  • Decathlon: 10 events drawn from WAG + MAG
  • Omnithon: shorter form, same idea
  • Exists only at UCG
UAG rules
T&T

Trampoline & Tumbling

Three sub-disciplines — individual trampoline, double-mini, and power tumbling — at every level from novice through advanced.

  • USAG T&T-compatible skill scoring
  • Compulsory and optional routines
  • Growing fast year over year
T&T rules
M

Masters

The bracket built for athletes returning to the sport, competing alongside fellow adults at any skill level. No pressure to qualify, no minimum age beyond 18 — just a welcoming format designed to keep you flipping.
