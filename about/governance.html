Elections

The next Board election cycle opens February 1, 2026, with voting through March 15 and new terms beginning at the Annual General Meeting at Nationals.

Open seats this cycle: 4 At-Large Directors, Treasurer, Secretary.

  • Nominations open: Feb 1 – Feb 28
  • Candidate Q&A: Mar 5
  • Voting: Mar 8 – Mar 15
  • Results: Mar 18
  • Terms begin: AGM at Nationals (Apr 10, 2026)
Run for the Board

Eligibility

Any current UCG individual member in good standing for at least 12 consecutive months may run for the Board. SafeSport training must be current. No active conflicts of interest as defined in our Conflict of Interest policy.
