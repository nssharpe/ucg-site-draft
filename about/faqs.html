Still stuck?

Email info@ucg.org — we typically respond within 48 hours.

Do I need to be in college to join UCG?

No. UCG is open to any adult 18 or older, whether or not you're a current student. We have a large community of post-collegiate, alumni, and never-in-college adult gymnasts.

Do I need to belong to a club to compete?

No. You can compete as an unattached individual at most sanctioned events. That said, most members find a local club enriches the experience — see our Clubs Directory.

How much does membership cost?

Individual membership is $55/year, club membership is $105/year, and coach membership is $45/year. Membership runs July 1 – June 30. See Membership for full details.

What skill level do I need to compete?

Any. UCG's level system spans Level 1 (true beginner) through Level 9 / Decathlon (essentially NCAA-level routines). You compete against athletes at your level.

Is there a maximum age beyond 18?

No upper limit. Our Masters bracket exists specifically to welcome athletes returning to the sport at any age.

How do I register for Nationals?

Registration happens through ScoreFlippers and opens in early winter each year. See the Nationals hub and Registration Help.

What if my school doesn't have a club?

Start one! We have a step-by-step playbook and a regional rep ready to help. Visit Start or Grow a Club.

Do you offer healthcare or insurance?

We offer complimentary specialty healthcare services (PT, chiropractic, massage, nutrition consults) at Nationals and at select sanctioned events. See Healthcare.

Are scholarships available?

Yes — three $2,000 scholarships annually: Spirit of Leadership, Underrepresented Athlete Scholarship, and the Rainbow Scholarship. See Scholarships.

How do I run a meet at my gym?

Step-by-step guidance is on our Host a Meet page, including how to get sanctioned and find judges.

Is UCG affiliated with USA Gymnastics?

UCG is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our rules are USAG-derived in WAG, MAG, and T&T, but we operate independently and have additional disciplines (UAG, Masters) that USAG does not offer.

Why did NAIGC rebrand to UCG?

We outgrew the 'Intercollegiate' label — many members aren't college students. UCG (United Club Gymnastics) better reflects who we actually serve. See Our Story for the full reasoning.
