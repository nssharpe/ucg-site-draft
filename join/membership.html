Membership.

$55/year individual · $109/year club · $45/year coach. Everything UCG opens up once you're in.

For athletes

Individual

$55/year

For anyone 18+ who wants to compete — regardless of student status, experience level, or club affiliation.

  • Compete at any sanctioned event
  • Compete at Nationals
  • Apply for UCG scholarships
  • Healthcare services at Nationals
  • Member sponsor discounts
  • Alumni network & communities

Membership runs July 1 – June 30. Non-refundable.

Register
For clubs

Club

$109/year

Required for a club to have its athletes compete as a team. Paid by the club admin.

  • Team competition at sanctioned events
  • Free sanctioning for one hosted meet/year
  • Bulk athlete registration
  • Club listing in directory
  • Regional rep support
  • Equipment discounts

Runs July 1 – June 30. Non-refundable.

For coaches

Coach

$45/year

For coaches working with UCG clubs — covers SafeSport-compliant access to the gym floor at sanctioned events.

  • Coach badge at sanctioned events
  • Floor access during competition
  • Listing in UCG coach directory
  • Discounts on judge certification
  • Coaching resources library

SafeSport certification required.

The fine print

You don't need to be in a club

Individual membership stands alone. You can compete as an unattached athlete at most sanctioned events, including Nationals.

You don't need to be a student

UCG was historically intercollegiate — that's why the previous name had "Intercollegiate" in it. We dropped that framing because more than a third of our active members are post-college adults.

SafeSport

All members 18+ must complete SafeSport training annually. The training is free, takes about 90 minutes, and is required before you can compete at any sanctioned event.

Renewals

You'll get an email in mid-June with a one-click renewal link. Renew before your membership lapses to avoid a brief gap in eligibility.
