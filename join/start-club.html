Start a club.

No UCG club near you? Start one. We'll send the playbook and pair you with a regional rep.

Starting a UCG club is more straightforward than people expect. Most clubs run on one or two committed founders, a coach willing to spot a few hours a week, and a place to practice.

The playbook

1. Find a place to practice

Most college clubs negotiate practice time at their school's varsity gymnastics gym, a local commercial gym, or (sometimes) their campus rec center. Community clubs typically rent time from a local gymnastics gym during off-hours.

2. Recruit a starting roster

You need ~6 athletes to make a club viable. Post on campus Facebook groups, your school's club fair, /r/gymnastics, local adult gymnastics meetups, and via word of mouth at any local gym.

3. Find a coach or willing spotter

A coach is helpful but not required at first. Many UCG clubs start with a small group of experienced gymnasts who spot each other. UCG can help connect you with coaches in your area.

4. Register your club with UCG

$105/year for club membership. Once you're registered, you get listed in the clubs directory and your athletes can compete as a team.

5. Talk to your regional rep

Your regional rep will help you find your first meets, connect you with nearby clubs, and answer the inevitable questions about insurance, liability, and gym access.

Resources we'll send you

  • Club starter playbook (PDF)
  • Sample constitution & bylaws
  • Insurance & liability primer
  • First-meet logistics checklist
  • Recruiting materials & flyer templates

Ready to start?

Tell us about your situation and we'll send the playbook plus connect you with your regional rep within a week.
