Donate.

Where your dollars go — and why most of them end up funding scholarships and lowering fees for athletes who need help.

Every dollar donated to UCG either goes to scholarships, lowers fees for athletes who need it, or directly subsidizes Nationals — the largest single line item in our budget.

Where donations go

Scholarship Fund (~55% of donations)

Three named $2,000 scholarships annually for undergraduate UCG members. The fund pays out exactly what's donated — no overhead, no admin fees taken. See Scholarships for details.

Athlete Assistance (~25%)

Direct support for members who couldn't otherwise afford membership, travel to Nationals, or sanctioned event registration. Confidential application; quick turnaround.

Nationals Subsidy (~15%)

Renting an arena, judges, equipment, and healthcare staff for four days is expensive. Donations keep Nationals registration affordable.

Operations (~5%)

Insurance, software, accounting, and the rest of the boring-but-required infrastructure of running a nonprofit.

Tax-deductible

UCG is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 12-3456789 (mock). Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You'll receive an automatic receipt after donating.

Other ways to give

  • Recurring monthly gift — even $5/month adds up.
  • Employer match — many employers will match your gift. Ask HR or check our match lookup.
  • Stock or DAF transfer — email finance@ucg.org.
  • Named scholarship endowment — start a $25k+ endowment to fund a scholarship in perpetuity. Contact finance@ucg.org.
  • Legacy gift — include UCG in your estate plan.
