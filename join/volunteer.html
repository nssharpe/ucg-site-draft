What volunteering looks like at UCG

It's collegial

Most OLT roles are 2–6 hours a week, done remotely. Nationals weekend is a sprint. You set the pace.

It compounds

Volunteers often grow into Board roles or paid clinic work. Many of our current Board members started on the OLT.

It's how we exist

UCG has one paid Executive Director. Everything else — including the people writing this page — is volunteer time.
