What is UCG?

The national home for collegiate and adult club gymnastics.

United Club Gymnastics — formerly NAIGC — is a volunteer-led nonprofit that's been running competitions, building community, and championing club gymnastics in the United States since 1989.

We sanction local meets, host the largest adult gymnastics championship in the country, write the rules our community competes by, fund scholarships, and connect more than 2,600 athletes across 150+ clubs nationwide.
